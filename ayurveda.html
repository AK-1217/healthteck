Science of Life

Ayurveda is the system of medicine that evolved in India with a rationale logical foundation and has been practiced worldwide. The term Ayurveda combines the Sanskrit words ayus (life) and veda (science or knowledge), meaning "the science of life."

The ancient Indian medical system known as Ayurveda is regarded as one of the oldest systems of medicine in the world. It emphasizes the balance of three doshas — Vata, Pitta, and Kapha — which correspond to the five elements (Panchamahabhuta) and govern physiological functions.

Key Principles

  • Doshas: Vata, Pitta, Kapha govern constitution and health.
  • Panchamahabhuta: Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Ether — the building blocks of matter.
  • Dhatus & Malas: Seven tissues and three waste products.
  • Updates & Panchakarma: Detoxification and rejuvenation therapies.

Classical Texts

The foundational texts include the Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita, and Ashtanga Hridaya. Later works such as the Bhavaprakasha and Sharangadhara Samhita expanded the pharmacopoeia and formulations.
