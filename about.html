Vision & Mission

Healthtech is an educational resource dedicated to the optimal understanding and appreciation of traditional systems of medicine — Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homoeopathy.

Our mission is to share knowledge about these systems, promote their safe and informed use, and help people discover classical remedies through our drug and formulation search tool.

About the Systems

The portal brings together information drawn from classical texts and recognized traditional medicine frameworks, including:

  • Ayurveda — the science of life based on the three doshas
  • Yoga & Naturopathy — harmony of body, mind and nature
  • Unani — the Greek-Arabic system of humoral medicine
  • Siddha — the ancient Tamil system of medicine
  • Homoeopathy — the system of "like cures like"

Objectives

  • Promote awareness of traditional systems of medicine.
  • Provide reliable references from classical texts.
  • Explain key principles, drugs, and formulations.
  • Support safe, educational exploration of natural remedies.
  • Encourage informed discussion of holistic healthcare.
