The Tamil System of Medicine

Siddha is one of the oldest systems of medicine, believed to have originated in South India, particularly Tamil Nadu. It is attributed to the Siddhars — enlightened saints who achieved perfection through spiritual discipline.

Siddha medicine closely corresponds to Ayurveda but has its own unique philosophy, emphasizing the balance of Vatha, Pitha, and Kapha and the use of herbs, minerals, and metals (including Rasa preparations).

Key Principles

  • Mukkutram: The three humors (Vatham, Pitham, Kabam).
  • Panchabootham: The five elements.
  • Uyir & Udal: Vital life force and body.
  • Rasa Vaidya: Use of metallic and mineral preparations.

Classical Texts

Siddha literature includes the works of Agastiyar, Theraiyar, and Bogar, comprising hundreds of texts on medicine, alchemy, and philosophy.

Quick Fact

Siddha is primarily practiced in Tamil Nadu and neighboring regions of South India and Sri Lanka.
