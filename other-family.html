About Other Families of Healing

Beyond the mainstream recognised systems, a rich tapestry of regional and allied traditional medicine families continues to serve communities across India. These families blend local knowledge, herbal lore, and time-tested healing practices.

  • Folk / Tribal Medicine: Community-based healing using local plants, minerals and rituals.
  • Naturopathy: A drugless system emphasising diet, fasting, water therapy and lifestyle correction.
  • Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM): An ancient Asian system using acupuncture and herbal formulae.
  • Japanese Kampo: A Japanese adaptation of traditional herbal medicine.
  • Global Herbal Traditions: Whole-plant herbal remedies and holistic wellness practices.
