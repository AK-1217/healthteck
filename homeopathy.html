The System of Simiia

Homoeopathy is a medical system based on the principle of "Similia Similibus Curentur" — "like cures like." It was developed by the German physician Samuel Hahnemann in the late 18th century.

Homoeopathy treats the individual as a whole, using highly diluted substances that in larger doses would produce symptoms similar to the disease. It emphasizes the body's vital force and self-healing capacity.

Key Principles

  • Similia Similibus Curentur: Like cures like.
  • Minimum Dose: Use of the smallest possible dose.
  • Individualization: Treatment based on the unique symptom picture.
  • Vital Force: The body's innate healing energy.

Miasms

Hahnemann introduced the concept of miasms (Psora, Sycosis, Syphilis) as chronic disease tendencies that predispose individuals to illness, guiding long-term treatment.

Quick Fact

India is one of the largest practitioners of Homoeopathy, with a dedicated Central Council and research institutes.
