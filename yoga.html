Harmonizing Body, Mind & Nature

Yoga is a group of physical, mental, and spiritual practices that originated in ancient India. Aimed at the integration of body, mind, and soul, yoga promotes health and wellbeing through postures (Asanas), breathing (Pranayama), and meditation (Dhyana).

Naturopathy is a drugless system of healing based on the theory that disease arises from the violation of nature's laws and that health is achieved through natural elements such as air, water, sunlight, and a wholesome diet.

Eight Limbs of Yoga (Ashtanga)

  • Yama — Ethical restraints
  • Niyama — Personal observances
  • Asana — Postures
  • Pranayama — Breath control
  • Pratyahara — Sense withdrawal
  • Dharana — Concentration
  • Dhyana — Meditation
  • Samadhi — Enlightenment

Naturopathy Card

Naturopathy emphasizes the body's innate power to heal itself through non-invasive therapies, including hydrotherapy, mud therapy, fasting, and dietetics.

International Yoga Day

June 21 is celebrated worldwide as the International Day of Yoga, following the UN declaration in 2014.
